How to test your webcam

Click Allow camera and give your browser permission to access your webcam. If your camera is working correctly, a live preview will appear above.

How do I know if my webcam is working?

If you can see a live image from your camera, your browser is successfully receiving video from the webcam. The tester also displays the video resolution reported by your camera.

Why does my webcam need permission?

Modern browsers protect camera access for privacy and security. A website must request permission before it can access your webcam.

My webcam isn't working. What should I do?

First check that your camera is connected and not being used exclusively by another application. Check your operating-system camera permissions and browser permissions, then try restarting the browser and testing the camera again.

Does DeviceTest record my webcam?

No. The webcam preview is processed locally in your browser. DeviceTest does not need to upload your camera video to a server to perform this test.

This online webcam test is a browser-based diagnostic tool. It does not install software or upload your camera video. Results can vary depending on your browser, operating system, webcam and device permissions.
