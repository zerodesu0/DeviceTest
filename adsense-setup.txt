ADSENSE SETUP
1. Replace every example.com URL with your real domain.
2. Create your Google AdSense account and add your site.
3. After approval, replace the visible "ADVERTISEMENT" placeholders with the exact AdSense code Google gives you.
4. Do not modify the AdSense code or encourage users to click ads.
5. Add your real contact email and adapt the privacy policy to your actual analytics/advertising vendors.
6. Test the site without ads first.

IMPORTANT: Google requires original, useful content and compliance with publisher policies. A tool alone is not a guarantee of approval.
