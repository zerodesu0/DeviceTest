How to test a touchscreen

Touch the testing area with one or more fingers. DeviceTest shows the number of active touch points and counts taps and swipe gestures reported by the browser.

What does a touchscreen test check?

This test checks whether your browser is receiving touch input. It can help identify an area of a screen that does not respond to touch, but it cannot diagnose every display or digitizer fault.

How do I test multi-touch?

Place two or more fingers on the test area at the same time. The touch-point counter should increase when your browser reports multiple active contacts.

Why isn't my touchscreen responding?

Clean the screen, remove accessories that may interfere with touch, restart the device and test another browser or app. If only one part of the screen consistently fails, the hardware may need service.
