How to use the Mouse Double Click Test

Click the test area twice quickly. DeviceTest measures the time between your clicks and detects whether they form a double click. You can repeat the test as many times as you want.

How do I know if my mouse is double clicking?

A mouse may have a double-click problem if you click once but your computer registers two clicks. This can cause files to open unexpectedly, text to be selected incorrectly or buttons to activate twice.

What causes mouse double clicking?

Double clicking can be caused by a worn or faulty mouse switch, electrical contact problems, dust or debris, or other hardware issues. Software and operating-system settings can also affect click behavior.

Can this test detect a faulty mouse switch?

This test can show how your browser is receiving mouse clicks, but it cannot directly inspect the physical switch inside your mouse. If your mouse repeatedly registers two clicks when you intentionally click once, the hardware may need further testing or replacement.

What is a normal double-click speed?

There is no single speed that is correct for every mouse. This test uses the time between two clicks to identify quick consecutive clicks, while your operating system and browser may use their own double-click timing settings.

Mouse Double Click Test FAQ

Why does my mouse double click when I only click once?

A worn mouse switch is a common cause, although software settings or other input problems can also produce unexpected clicks. Try testing the mouse with another computer or browser to help determine whether the problem is specific to your current setup.

How can I test my mouse double click speed?

Click the test area twice as quickly as you normally would. DeviceTest measures the time between the two clicks and displays the interval in milliseconds.

Can I test a gaming mouse for double clicking?

Yes. The test works with standard mice and gaming mice as long as your browser receives their button input.

Does this mouse test require a download?

No. The test runs directly in your web browser and does not require additional software.
