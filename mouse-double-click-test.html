Try the test several times. If your mouse sometimes registers one physical click as two clicks, you may notice unexpected double-click detections.

What is a mouse double click?

A double click is two mouse clicks performed in quick succession. Double clicking is commonly used to open files, launch applications and perform other actions on a computer.

Why is my mouse double clicking?

If your mouse registers two clicks when you only intended to click once, the mouse switch may be worn or faulty. Software settings, mouse drivers and operating-system configuration can also affect mouse behavior.

Can this test detect a faulty mouse switch?

This test cannot physically inspect the switch inside your mouse. It can show you how your browser receives the mouse click events, which can help identify unusual clicking behavior.

Mouse Double Click Test FAQ

How do I test my mouse for double clicking?

Click the testing area twice quickly. The test will detect and count the double click. Repeat it several times to check consistency.

Why is my mouse double clicking when I click once?

This can happen because of a worn mouse switch, hardware problems, mouse software or operating-system settings. Testing the mouse on another computer can help determine whether the problem follows the mouse.

Can a website test mouse double clicking?

Yes. A browser can detect mouse click events and measure the time between clicks. However, a website cannot directly inspect the physical switch inside the mouse.

How can I fix a mouse that double clicks?

Try another USB port or computer and check your mouse software and operating-system settings. If the problem continues on other computers, the physical mouse switch may be worn or faulty.
