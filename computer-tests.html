ABOUT THESE TESTS

What DeviceTest can check

Test common computer hardware directly in your browser. Check your mouse, double clicks, keyboard, controller, microphone, webcam, speakers and browser-reported device information. The available information depends on your browser, operating system and hardware. Some tools require permission before the browser can access a camera or microphone.

These tests are diagnostic helpers rather than hardware certification tools. If a test fails, try another browser, reconnect the device, check operating-system permissions and test the hardware on another device when possible.
