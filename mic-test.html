How to test your microphone

Click Allow microphone and give your browser permission to access your microphone. Then speak or make a sound. The live meter shows the microphone's detected input level.

How do I know if my microphone is working?

If the input level increases when you speak, your browser is receiving audio from the selected microphone. If the meter stays at zero, check your microphone connection, operating-system input settings and browser permissions.

Why does the microphone need permission?

Web browsers protect microphone access for privacy and security. A website cannot normally access your microphone without asking you for permission first.

Is my microphone recording uploaded?

No. DeviceTest analyzes the microphone signal locally in your browser. This test does not send your microphone audio to a DeviceTest server.

This online microphone test is a browser-based diagnostic tool. It does not save or upload your microphone recording. Results can vary depending on your browser, operating system, microphone and device permissions.
