PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

At DeviceTest, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the DeviceTest website and its browser-based device testing tools.

Device tests

DeviceTest provides browser-based tests for devices such as mice, keyboards, microphones, webcams and gamepads.

Basic device tests are designed to run directly in your browser. DeviceTest does not intentionally upload your mouse, keyboard or gamepad test data to our servers.

Microphone and webcam permissions

Some DeviceTest features require permission to access your microphone or webcam. Your browser will ask for permission before these devices can be used.

The microphone and webcam tests are designed to process the relevant device input locally in your browser. DeviceTest does not intentionally record, store or upload your microphone or webcam content through these tests.

Advertising

DeviceTest may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure and improve advertising. These technologies may allow advertising providers to process information such as browser type, device information, approximate location and interactions with advertisements.

Google may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website and other websites. Users may learn more about how Google uses information for advertising through Google's advertising policies and settings.

Cookies

DeviceTest itself does not require cookies for the basic device testing features to function.

Third-party services used on the website, such as advertising or analytics services, may use cookies or similar technologies. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. Some website features or advertising-related functionality may be affected by these settings.

Information we collect

DeviceTest does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic device testing tools.

If you contact us, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message. This information is used to respond to your request.

Third-party services

DeviceTest may use third-party services for purposes such as website hosting, advertising, security or analytics. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

DeviceTest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The website does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use the basic testing tools.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DeviceTest adds new features, services or advertising providers. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how DeviceTest handles information, please contact us through our Contact page.
